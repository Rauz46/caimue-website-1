Our Story

A simple belief

Caimue started with a simple belief: great stories can come from anywhere. We began on YouTube, producing original Malayalam and Tamil short films, written, cast and directed in-house, and grew past 2 million views one honest story at a time.

Something bigger than films

Along the way we discovered something bigger than films: people. More than 25 writers, directors and actors earned their first real credits with us, and our channels became homes for regional stories and founder journeys told with cinematic craft.

Today

Today that same craft powers a video-first B2B marketing agency: corporate videos, LinkedIn media campaigns and marketing websites built with the same speed and soul. Our short films and features stay part of the story too, a proving ground for the talent behind every brand we work with.
